# AL ANJUM Corporate Website

## Build
- Create a single-page, conversion-focused website for AL ANJUM Accounting and Bookkeeping Co. LLC, using the supplied logo and its black/gold palette with a restrained complementary teal accent.
- Preserve the requested information flow: sticky navigation, editorial hero, trust placeholders, introduction, accounting services, featured solutions, four-step process, differentiators, placeholder statistics, success stories, testimonials, insights, FAQ, final call-to-action, contact form, and professional footer.
- Adapt broad consultancy examples to accounting and bookkeeping: bookkeeping, VAT, corporate tax, payroll, CFO support, audit preparation, compliance, and company finance setup.

## Content safeguards
- Keep unprovided phone, email, address, hours, client names, metrics, awards, certifications, testimonials, and prices clearly marked as placeholders.
- Use neutral logo/certification placeholders rather than fabricated brands.
- Treat the Smart Zone website only as structural and conversion inspiration; all design, wording, and imagery will be original.

## Experience
- Add responsive desktop/mobile navigation, smooth anchor scrolling, restrained reveal motion, animated placeholder counters, testimonial controls, accessible FAQ accordions, validated enquiry form with success state, floating WhatsApp placeholder, and back-to-top control.
- Build from reusable sections and content arrays so services, stories, articles, testimonials, and statistics are easy to replace.
- Add route-specific search/social metadata and Organization/ProfessionalService structured data without inventing missing business details.

## Visual direction
- Use a charcoal and near-white editorial foundation, metallic gold from the logo, and deep teal as the complementary accent.
- Use strong modern sans-serif typography, crisp grids, selective rounded cards, thin rules, subtle glass treatment, and high-quality original UAE business imagery.
- Keep gradients and decorative motion minimal; prioritize credibility, readability, and lead conversion.

## Validation
- Verify interactions and layout at desktop and mobile widths, confirm no placeholder template remains, and check the final metadata and page structure.
